 Advantages of PDF digital signatures PDF digital signatures provide several benefits over traditional signatures, including: 
 Security 
 With cryptography, PDF digital signatures guarantee the authenticity and integrity of the signed document, ensuring high levels of security. 
 Convenience 
 With the ability to sign PDF documents from any location -provided you have internet access- there is no need for printing or scanning. 
 Speed 
 The digital signature process is quick and easy. 
 Cost savings 
 Digital signatures eliminate the expenses associated with printing, mailing and storing physical documents, leading to significant cost reductions. 
 How to sign a PDF document with a digital certificate? 
 Step 1: Create account 
 To get started, create an account on our platform. You can start now by registering for our 7-day free trial . It’s not necessary for the signer to have an account with Signaturit, only the sender. 
 Step 2: Set up PDF document 
 Upload the PDF document on our platform, and add the fields that you want the signer to fill in: name, date, signature, etc. 
 Step 3: Send PDF document 
 Once you’ve set up the PDF document, you just need to enter the name and email address of the signer, and add a personalised message. 
 Step 4: Signer receives signature request 
 The recipient receives an email or SMS with a link, which they can click on to open the document, using any device. No need to download an app! 
 Step 5: Electronically sign PDF document 
 The recipient signs the document by drawing their signature with a mouse, pen, or finger, depending on the device. Once the process is validated, both the sender and signer will receive a copy of the signed document.
